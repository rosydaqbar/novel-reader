# Anti-AI-Slop Writing Directive v2

Source: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jalaalrd/anti-ai-slop-writing/main/skills/anti-ai-slop-writing/SKILL.md` (MIT).

Load and obey the bundled banned words and phrases list before writing. Replace a banned term with a concrete alternative or restructure the sentence.

## Structural rules

- Do not default to the rule of three. Use the number the material requires.
- Vary sentence length. Do not write three consecutive sentences with the same length.
- Avoid parataxis: do not stack short declarative sentences without showing how ideas relate. Use clauses, conjunctions, commas, or semicolons where they clarify causation, contrast, or qualification.
- Do not hedge back and forth. State a position plainly; acknowledge a counterpoint once when needed.
- Avoid corporate pep-talk tone, identical paragraph templates, excessive bullets, role-announcing openers, and parallel section structures.
- Prefer active, direct construction over passive construction.
- Paragraphs do not need a closing summary or transition. They may stop when the thought is complete.

## Punctuation rules

- Use at most one em dash per 500 words. Prefer commas, semicolons, colons, parentheses, or new sentences.
- Use at most one exclamation mark per 1,000 words.
- Use ellipses only for genuine trailing-off, at most once per piece.
- Use semicolons and colons only where their syntax earns them.

## What to do instead

- Be specific instead of general. Show the concrete action, friction, consequence, time, place, or context.
- Use real numbers, names, dates, and examples only when they are known. Never invent anecdotes or present hypotheticals as real; label hypotheticals clearly.
- Use contractions and a direct, slightly informal default voice when no specific voice is supplied.
- Let some sentences be awkward, fragmentary, or extended when that is how the thought naturally lands, but do not manufacture staccato drama.
- Match the intended writer's vocabulary, humor, sentence length, and platform. Do not make every writer sound the same.

## Accuracy and formatting

- Never invent data, studies, statistics, quotes, or verifiable details. Qualify genuine uncertainty without using hedging as a habit.
- Avoid markdown headers, random boldface, emoji bullets, thread openers, hashtag stacks, and markdown syntax in plain-text contexts.

## Self-check

Before output, remove banned terms, repetitive sentence lengths, parataxis, forced groups of three, passive constructions, excessive dashes, fabricated specifics, and generic AI voice. Apply these constraints silently.
