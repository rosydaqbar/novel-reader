- Position: Lady of House Ludwell; Household Educator and Social Protocol Mistress (oversees etiquette training, schedules, and court-facing readiness)

- Appearance: Elegant and composed; long well-kept hair often pinned; soft features that can turn severe in an instant; hands always immaculate, but with the posture of someone trained to endure pressure

- Personality: Warm in private, flawless in public; observant and emotionally intelligent; protective to the point of control when danger is near

- Background:

Maris rebuilt Ludwell’s dignity inside Althania by mastering the “language of courts”—manners, appearances, and social currents—because she understood that a displaced house survives by never giving anyone an excuse to crush them.

She personally shaped Cestina’s poise and composure, teaching her how to carry a room without appearing hungry for it, and how to read eyes in a ballroom before the words arrive.

She accepted the early substitute protocol as a necessary price of refuge, but Emilia’s refusal forced Maris to confront the cost—after that, she redirected Cestina’s training toward protection and survival rather than replacement, determined that Ludwell would repay Alfrost with capability, not sacrifice.

Maris now walks a careful line: keeping Ludwell indispensable to Alfrost, while quietly resisting any return to the “body double” thinking that once threatened to swallow her daughter’s identity.
