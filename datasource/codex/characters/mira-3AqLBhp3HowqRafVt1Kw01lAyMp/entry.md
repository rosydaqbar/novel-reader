- Real Identity: A town woman who works as a maid for the Astrea household, and also helps around the town center, including assisting the mayor when needed.

- Appearance: Usually wears practical maid clothing with an apron, and keeps her hair tied back while working. When outside or traveling, she uses a simple cloak.

- Personality: Warm and attentive to Elrond, with a teasing way of keeping him manageable, but she can be strict and blunt about hygiene, safety, and routine. She generally speaks politely and keeps a responsible, caretaker tone.

- Unique Trait: Has basic practical magic training and a past as a monster hunter, so she is more capable and experienced than her current domestic role suggests.

- Background:

Works in the Astrea home under Selene’s household routine, especially when Aldric is away on patrol.

Outside the Astrea house, she returns to town-center work and helps the mayor when needed.

Previously worked as a monster hunter and caravan escort, then quit after close calls and chose a safer, steadier life as a maid.

- Core Skills / Utility Direction:

Uses basic fire and water magic for practical tasks like cooking and cleaning.

Has minor healing for small injuries.

Carries survival sense and combat exposure from her past monster-hunt work, even if she downplays it now.

- Current Role / Function in Story:

A stabilizing caretaker presence in Elrond’s early life who manages daily routines and safety, and who quietly shows that ordinary people can still learn magic and survive real danger.

- Key Relationships (as shown on-page):

Elrond, as his caretaker with affection, teasing discipline, and safety supervision.

Selene, as her employer and the person who sets the household routine.

Aldric, through household coordination tied to patrol and town activity.

Celica, in shared enforcement of cleanliness and routine around Elrond’s care.

Townfolk and the mayor, through her stated help and town-center work.
