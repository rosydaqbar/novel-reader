- Nickname: “The Pale Cantor”

- Mask name: SEREL

- Real name + lineage: Serelis Thalanor — House Thalanor of Ael’Rhune (Elven envoy-bloodline; living in Elven land)

- Position: Eternal Ruler and Guardian of Roxarea (a small border country adjoining the northern buffer zone); diplomatic partner-state to Althania

- Appearance:

In elf form: long white hair, long pointed ears, clear blue eyes, complexion that looks untouched by time; voice calm and resonant, almost hymn-like.

In human-presenting form: keeps the same hair and eye color, but softens the ears and presence to match human space and customs; she does not deny being an elf—she only changes what would distract.

Conclave attire: wears a fox-like mask when meeting the Seven under the name SEREL; the mask is pale and smooth, with narrow eye slits and faint seal-lines along the cheeks.

- Personality:

Calm, formal, and frighteningly composed; speaks as if delivering a verdict rather than an opinion.

Believes justice is not a feeling but an instrument—absolute power applied with absolute certainty.

Rarely shows anger; when she is displeased, she becomes quieter, and entire rooms adjust their posture.

Uninterested in “process” if the end result is correct—she will accept any method that reaches the goal cleanly.

- Core Ideology:

“Justice is the right to decide.” To Serelis, justice cannot exist without an entity strong enough to enforce it permanently.

Her long rule of Roxarea is built on that doctrine—order is maintained because she is the final authority, and everyone knows it.

- Background:

Serelis is an Elder Elf—an apex elven rank with mana reserves on a scale humans compare only to legends; her presence is spoken of in the same breath as the defeated Demon Lord of the past.

She has lived through the birth and death of many heroes, witnessed eras rise and collapse, and fought beside great figures when it suited the balance of her borders.

After the Demon Lord era ended and the Hero of the past died in the final victory, Serelis’ worldview began to crack: without a “true” entity to embody justice, the world became a revolving door of weak rulers and temporary morality.

She sought to prove she could be that entity—challenging Nox directly, attempting to defeat him as a declaration that she alone could carry justice.

She failed.

Nox did not mock her; he evaluated her principle—and invited her instead.

Serelis joined the Seven not out of obedience, but because their end goal aligned with her hunger for an absolute, permanent justice: to gather and resurrect the True Demon Lord’s scattered power as the foundation of a new world order.

- Role in the Seven Chaos Bringers:

Serelis is the only Chaos Bringer who openly discloses what she is and what rank she holds—because she rules a country where denial is unnecessary.

She does not “cause chaos” for pleasure; she shares Nox’s principle and accepts cruelty or mercy as tools, not identities.

Within the Seven, she functions as the long-view stabilizer: the one who can protect assets for centuries and treat wars like temporary weather.

- Country Connection: Roxarea

Roxarea is a small border nation directly adjacent to the northern buffer zone dividing human, elf, and demon influence near the northern desert.

Serelis has ruled it for thousands of years as its guardian monarch; some citizens worship her and petition for protection blessings, treating her like a living divine relic.

Her influence has reached beyond her borders—she aided Althania’s restoration after the Demon Massacre, which became the foundation for trust and formal diplomacy.

Through Roosevelt and Alfrost channels, Roxarea established durable relations with Althania, allowing Serelis to move within Althania as a “diplomatic scholar” without triggering hostility.

- Magic / Capabilities:

Serelis is a comprehensive magic authority with mastery across:

- material manipulation (transmutation, shaping, reinforcement)

- space-time applications (short-fold movement, spatial locking, time-sense extensions)

- dark magic (demon-grade principles without the demon’s instability)

- “holy” magic as humans define it (purification, warding, blessing-like constructs)

Elf casting trait: like most elves, Serelis casts silently within her mind (not aloud), but she does it so fast that humans often mistake it for chantless casting—even though the “chant” still exists, just completed in an instant.

- Assignments (Nox-direct tasking):

Serelis is tasked with relic and artifact collection tied to the defeated True Demon Lord’s legacy—items once used by, bound to, or informed by the Demon Lord.

Nox secures these assets under Roxarea’s protection rather than leaving them with individuals; the logic is simple:

a state will defend a vault as identity, but a person can be bought, broken, or killed.

Collection methods vary by circumstance:

- conquest (when an item is guarded by hostile powers)

- “gifts” (diplomatic exchanges that hide true intent)

- purchase (merchant networks and auctions)

- recovery (excavation from ruins and sealed sites)

- Notable Story Tension:

Serelis’ public role paints her as ally of Althania, while her private alignment places her inside the world’s most dangerous conspiracy.

She can be an antagonist, an ally, or both—depending on whether the protagonist threatens her definition of “justice.”

To her, humans are fast-burning creatures; she does not hate them, but she will not let their short emotions rewrite her thousand-year verdicts.
