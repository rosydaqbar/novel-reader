- Relation: Elrond’s mother

- Origin: Former noblewoman

- Occupation: Magic researcher

- Appearance: Light chestnut hair, gentle amber eyes, and a calm smile that hides quiet melancholy

- Personality: Kind, intelligent, and compassionate; prioritizes others over herself

- Background:

Selene was once part of a minor noble family in the capital, gifted in magical theory and elemental research. However, her love for Aldric, a commoner at the time, caused deep conflict within her household.

When her family forbade their union, Selene renounced her noble title and left her home—cutting all ties and swearing never to reveal her lineage again, as her family considered her existence a disgrace.

Now living under the Astrea name, Selene works as a magic researcher for the town, developing barrier systems and agricultural enchantments to improve local life.

Though her combat abilities are limited, her intelligence and empathy made her a cornerstone of the town’s well-being. Many locals see her as the heart of the community, quietly helping others without expecting anything in return.

Her curiosity and kindness greatly shaped Elrond’s intellectual and moral foundation, making her one of the most influential people in his life.
