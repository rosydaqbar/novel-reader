- Position: Emilia’s main maid (personal attendant + bodyguard-in-practice)

- Appearance: Ash-brown hair usually braided tight; gray-green eyes that rarely blink; slim but clearly trained (wrist guards under sleeves)

- Personality: Quietly strict, painfully efficient, loyal to the point of stubbornness; shows care through routine (warm tea, measured words, silent presence)

- Background:

Emilia is known to be composed and cautious, hiding loneliness behind maturity.

Eira was assigned not just to “serve,” but to become Emilia’s daily shield—walking half a step behind, watching hands, exits, and reflections.

She was recruited from a border-town security school after surviving an attempted kidnapping in her youth; House Alfrost values strategy and stability, so even their household staff are built like contingency plans.

Eira handles Emilia’s schedule, wardrobe, and letters—while quietly enforcing the “no blind corner” rule in every corridor of the mansion.

- Notable Ability:

Needleblade Technique — uses hairpins/sewing needles like concealed daggers; precise enough to disable without bloodshed.
