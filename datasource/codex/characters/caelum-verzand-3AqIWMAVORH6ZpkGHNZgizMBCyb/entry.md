- Family Name / Origin: House Varzend (newly ennobled line from Radras; foreign-rooted, not Althania-born)

- Position: Envoy-Noble of Radras; “Blessed Heir” whose household offers protection contracts to minor nobles seeking shelter

- Appearance: Tall and well-built without looking bulky; clean silver-black hair; clear blue eyes that feel like they’re measuring distance and motive; always dressed too neatly for travel, as if he expects an audience at any hour

- Personality: Calm, observant, effortlessly charismatic; outwardly disciplined and courteous, inwardly restless and bored—drawn to danger and unusual people simply to feel something real again

- Background:

Divine Blessings (artificial gift; not born with him):

But all of that blessing is uncontrollable, it's something that's activate on its own or it always active,

1) Blessing of Protection

- Nullifies incoming attacks without counterplay, except against other blessings.

2) Blessing of Courage

- Monsters and unintelligent beings instinctively fear him and hesitate to approach.

3) Blessing of Clairvoyance

- Sees and analyzes magical structures and intent, including malice and deception.

4) Blessing of Wisdom

- Vast mana reserves and unusually stable control.

5) Blessing of Might

- Physical strength and agility far beyond normal without spending mana.

Origin of the Blessings:

When Caelum was three, an unknown traveler branded him with five blessings as a “gift”—a deliberate construction meant to prepare a human vessel capable of containing a True Elder Dragon, a calamity-class descendant of a primordial dragon that has existed since the earliest era.

The traveler’s identity remains hidden in the present, though the truth points to a fallen Divine Being moving pieces for a larger scheme.

Current Reputation:

Caelum is treated as a rising “Hero candidate” by rumor alone; many minor nobles and anxious houses seek shelter under House Varzend’s protection because his presence changes the security balance in any region he occupies.

Despite that, his standing in Althania remains politically limited—Radras blood makes him an outsider in the princes’ succession struggle.

Plot Connection:

Through Clairvoyance, Caelum becomes one of the few who can sense Elrond’s abnormal mana without being told, and he attaches himself to Elrond with unusual interest—part friendship, part fascination—because Elrond feels like the first unpredictable thing in his life.

The Second Prince’s faction exploits him through promises of formal palace recognition for House Varzend and pressures him into a “romance approach” toward Emilia, believing that securing her would massively strengthen their legitimacy and alliances.

Caelum goes along with it less out of loyalty and more out of curiosity—he wants to see what breaks, what bends, and what surprises him—but a quiet fear sits behind the boredom: he suspects he’s walking toward a future disaster of his own making, and that Elrond may be the only person capable of pulling him back once the madness starts.
