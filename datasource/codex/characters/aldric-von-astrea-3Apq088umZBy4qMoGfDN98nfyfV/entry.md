- Relation: Elrond’s father

- Occupation: Captain of the town guard

- Appearance: Broad-shouldered, dark brown hair with faint streaks of gray, strong eyes filled with warmth and discipline

- Personality: Stoic, dependable, protective; holds deep respect for strength and justice

- Background:

Aldric is a renowned warrior in the outskirts town of Althania, often called the “Shield of the South” by locals. Though born into a common family, his strength and leadership earned him the title of Captain of the town guard, responsible for defending the region against monsters and bandits.

Despite lacking noble blood, his courage and moral conviction made him a respected figure among both commoners and wandering adventurers.

Aldric’s belief in earning one’s worth through effort deeply influenced Elrond’s personality. While humble and loyal, he hides a past of hardship—having once fought in the northern frontier war where he nearly lost his life.
