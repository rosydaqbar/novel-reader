- Position: Secondary maid (caretaker); responsible for Emilia’s health, rest, and private room upkeep

- Appearance: Warm brown hair in a loose bun; soft eyes; hands marked by burns and herb stains; always carries a linen pouch of dried leaves

- Personality: Gentle, motherly, and brave in the quiet way; talks little, listens a lot; the only maid who can make Emilia actually eat when she refuses

- Background:

Brina used to be a field-medic assistant during monster raids on rural routes; she learned to treat wounds fast, keep people calm, and move them before panic spreads.

She joined the Alfrost household after saving a minor vassal’s child during a carriage incident—her reward was not money, but placement in a house that protects its own.

Brina isn’t an assassin-hunter like the others, but she’s the one who notices subtle changes: pale lips, trembling hands, insomnia—she catches “collapse” before it becomes an event.

- Notable Ability:

Counter-Toxin Craft — specializes in antidotes, fever suppressants, and “calming drafts” that don’t dull the mind.
