- Position: Head of House Ashfeldt (Alfrost vassal); Commander of Ashfeldt Escort and Estate Security

- Appearance: Broad-shouldered, weathered face, short RED hair with early gray at the sides; old scars on hands/forearms; wears practical noble attire that still resembles a soldier’s uniform

- Personality: Direct, disciplined, protective; slow to trust, fast to act; shows care through training and preparedness rather than words

- Background:

Garrick’s house keeps its place by guarding Alfrost interests—routes, caravans, estates, and high-risk court movements—so he raised Ashfeldt with a soldier’s mindset.

He approved Lisoletelle’s assignment near Emilia not as “servant duty,” but as an honor-post: it strengthens Ashfeldt’s bond to Alfrost and keeps Emilia alive.
