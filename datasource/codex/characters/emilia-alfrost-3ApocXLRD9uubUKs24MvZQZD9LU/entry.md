- Family: Main branch of House Alfrost (Founding Family); court-adjacent nobility with long-standing ties to the Arbalest royal household

- Age: 15 (current timeline)

- Position: Heir-side “core” of House Alfrost (raised as the house’s public face alongside Gilbert; trained for both governance and combat)

- Appearance: Long, voluminous light-blue hair with pale white streaks; dark green eyes; very fair skin; composed, almost “too steady” posture for her age

- Personality (deeper): Intelligent and sharply observant; disciplined, low-drama, and bluntly honest when it matters

- Shows affection through practical actions (checking on injuries, remembering small preferences, quietly stepping in when someone is cornered)

- Carries a “responsibility-first” mindset that can read as distant, but people close to her recognize it as reliability

- Loneliness presents as guardedness: she rarely asks for comfort directly, but she clings tightly (quietly) to the few bonds she trusts

- Caution is habitual, not performative—she tracks timing, exits, and shifts in mood because she’s learned what “unsafe” looks like in noble life

- Background (expanded):

- Raised inside the Alfrost estate under a strict, structured routine (early mornings, training blocks, etiquette, then household/administrative exposure)

- Trained early by high-level instructors: sword forms and footwork under Julian Roosevelt’s guidance; ice-magic fundamentals under Lady Vespera’s supervision

- A major turning point was an assassination attempt during a city carriage ride when she was young; the aftermath hardened the household’s security culture and accelerated her “forced maturity”

- By twelve, she was already reading and discussing real governance documents with Caelen (trade requests, patrol reports, tax adjustments), trained to think like the person who signs the final order

- At fifteen, she’s no longer “studying” noble duties in theory: she processes and signs minor estate decisions herself, audits spending/tax adjustments for inconsistencies, and escalates anything politically sensitive to Gilbert/Caelen

- Maintains a tight friend-circle with Princess Claire, Cestina, and Lisoletelle; she’s noticeably more human with them—quieter humor, softer tone, and visible relief when they’re together

- Combat & magic profile:

- Weapon: sword (form-focused, clean technique; trained to prioritize stability/footwork over flash)

- Magic affinity: ice magic (high difficulty element; emphasizes precision and mental “shape control”)

- Common tendency under stress: over-reinforcing spells (pushing excess mana into a construct to feel “safe”), something Vespera drills out of her through repeated shaping exercises

- Strengths: composure under pressure; administrative competence; pattern-reading; high accountability; subtle leadership (she stabilizes rooms without needing to dominate them)

- Weaknesses: internalizes blame; struggles to relax; distrustful by default outside her inner circle; can overwork and become emotionally “flat” when overloaded

- Key relationships:

- Caelen Alfrost: primary model for duty and accountability; she learns leadership as “take responsibility first”

- Gilbert Alfrost: older brother and the house’s operational spearhead; she competes with him quietly and uses him as her reality-check for politics

- Cestina Ludwell: closest friend/confidante; pushes Emilia to face training and obligations instead of avoiding them

- Lisoletelle Ashfeldt: emotional counterbalance; keeps Emilia grounded and socially “alive”

- Princess Claire: genuine friendship inside a court environment where most relationships are transactional

- Julian Roosevelt: sword mentor figure; a key influence on her discipline and standards

- Lady Vespera: magic instructor; refines Emilia’s control and exposes her bad habits early

- Current trajectory: preparing for the Royal Academy entrance period while already functioning as a working part of House Alfrost’s governance machine (and trying to keep the people around her from becoming “collateral” in noble politics)
