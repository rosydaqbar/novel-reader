- Relation: Cassian’s son; heir-in-training

- Appearance: Short dark hair, bright gold-brown eyes; always ink-stained fingers; dresses too neatly for his age

- Personality: Curious, quick with numbers, bluntly honest; tries to imitate adult manners but slips into childish excitement when he sees rare items

- Background:

Lucio is being raised as a “little accountant prince”—taught ledgers, weights, quality grading, and contract language before swordplay.

He has a small talent for appraisal: he notices inconsistencies (fake seals, mismatched materials, tampered crates) faster than most adults.

- Story Use:

A harmless-looking child who can accidentally uncover sabotage, corruption, or an assassination attempt hidden inside “normal” deliveries.
