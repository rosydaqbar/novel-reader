- Title: Second Prince of the Althania Kingdom

- Age: Early 17s

- Appearance: Bright yellow hair and blue eyes, resembling the King more than his siblings

- Personality: Intelligent, ambitious, and proud; often impulsive and driven by emotion

- Background:

Darius is the second prince, talented in both sword and magic but overshadowed by his elder brother Lucien. Though not chosen as heir, he seeks recognition through his strength and influence.

Fueled by frustration and ambition, Darius formed the Second Prince Faction, attracting nobles who shared his vision of radical progress and personal gain. Unlike Lucien, who values diplomacy, Darius believes that power and control are the true path to leadership.

His fascination with power led him down a darker path—secretly studying forbidden magic and gathering individuals blessed with powerful Divine Blessings under his influence.

He views the Divine Blessing not as a divine gift, but as a tool—a means to rule and reshape the kingdom under his own image.

His growing network of Blessed individuals and forbidden scholars has become a source of unease within the royal court, with rumors that his faction’s power rivals even the royal knights.

Though misguided, Darius genuinely believes his actions are for the kingdom’s future, convinced that only through dominance can true order be achieved.
