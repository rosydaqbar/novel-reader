- Age: 20

- Origin World: Earth

- Occupation (before death): Mathematician

- Traits: Photographic memory, analytical, perfectionist

- Cause of Death: Overwork at his desk after realizing his research led to humanity’s downfall

- Background:

Saitou was a young mathematical genius devoted to solving the most complex equations of human advancement. His groundbreaking work in theoretical mathematics became the foundation for a revolutionary new energy system—one later twisted into a devastating weapon of mass destruction.

The same discovery meant to guide humanity into prosperity instead annihilated much of his world. Overwhelmed by guilt, Saitou continued his research endlessly, desperate to find a way to reverse the damage. Exhausted and broken, he eventually collapsed at his desk.

Out of pity and respect, the Divine Beings offered him a second chance—to live again in another world, not as a punishment, but as an opportunity to rebuild his life free from the burden of his past mistakes.
