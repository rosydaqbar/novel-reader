- Occupation: Mentor to Elrond and Gilbert

- Family: Roosevelt — noble house of scholars and swordsmen, known for mastering both magic and martial discipline

- Special Gift: Divine Blessing of Space Manipulation

- Appearance: Shoulder-length dark red hair, calm and elegant presence

- Personality: Thoughtful, patient, never wastes a move

- Background:

The Roosevelt family is one of Althania’s few houses excelling in both magic and swordsmanship, earning them a reputation as dual-discipline masters.

Julian is a rare talent who inherited this balance perfectly—an accomplished mage who wields his blade with equal precision.

Recognized Elrond’s hidden potential early and took him as an apprentice. His mastery of spatial magic and refined sword techniques make him one of the most formidable tacticians in the kingdom.
