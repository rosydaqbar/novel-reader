- Position: Joren Caldra’s underling (covert enforcer / battlefield “control” specialist)

- Nickname (public rumor): “The Marionettist of Flesh”

- Cover Identity: Traveling healer-artisan (stitchwork, prosthetics, “rehabilitation therapy”)

- Age: Late 20s

- Appearance: Long raven hair tied back, pale skin, fingers always wrapped in thin bandages; calm eyes that look more clinical than cruel

- Personality: Soft-spoken, eerily polite, treats bodies like “systems”; never angry—only disappointed when things are inefficient

- Signature Magic: Flesh Puppetcraft

- Core effect: Can seize control of living or dead targets by imbuing her mana into the body, then casting the puppet spell.

- Cost rule: The stronger the target (willpower, mana, physique), the more mana she must implant before control is possible.

- Imbue methods:

1) Direct Imbue: physical contact or close-range injection of mana (fast, risky).

2) Clash Imbue: she forces an existing puppet to “clash” with a new target; each clash transfers a portion of her mana into the target, building the required imprint step-by-step (safer, slower, more theatrical).

- Control profile:

- Living targets retain reflexive resistance; dead targets are easier but “stiffer” and less adaptive.

- Partial control is possible (one limb, breathing, speech) before full domination—depending on how much mana is imbued.

- Reputation in Althania:

Known in whispers from incidents where guards “turned on their own,” corpses moved after battle, and witnesses swore they saw someone’s body fight like it wasn’t theirs.

- Weakness / Limit:

Her craft is mana-hungry and punishes her nerves—overuse causes tremors and numbness in her hands, and if her imbued mana is disrupted, control snaps violently (often injuring the puppet body).

- Story Role:

Joren uses Mirelle when he needs outcomes without visible assassins—turning security into liability, forcing “accidents,” and making wars uglier without leaving a blade to blame.
