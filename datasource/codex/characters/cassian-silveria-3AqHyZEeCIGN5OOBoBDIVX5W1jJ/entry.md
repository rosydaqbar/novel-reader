- Title: Head of House Silveria; Royal Procurement Liaison (contracts, supplies, logistics)

- Age: Early 40s

- Appearance: Well-groomed dark hair, neat beard shadow, amber eyes; always carries a slim ledger-book and a wax-seal kit

- Personality: Polite and smiling in public, razor-sharp in negotiation; hates waste, values reliability over pride

- Background:

Cassian built House Silveria’s influence by controlling “unromantic necessities”—grain routes, iron orders, medicine stocks, and transport permits—so even warrior houses must speak to him when they need things to actually arrive on time.

He avoids direct combat, but treats procurement like warfare: redundancy, quiet audits, and never trusting a single supplier.

- Story Use:

The man who can save a campaign with a signature… or ruin a rival by “delaying” the right shipment.
