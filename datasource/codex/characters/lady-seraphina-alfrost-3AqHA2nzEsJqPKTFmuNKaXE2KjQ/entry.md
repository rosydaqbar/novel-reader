- Relation: Emilia & Gilbert’s late mother (deceased)

- Title: Former Lady of House Alfrost; Patron of Hospice Wards (private foundation)

- Age (at death): Early 40s

- Appearance: Long black hair with pale white streaks (the trait later seen in Emilia); gentle green eyes; fragile elegance in later years

- Personality: Soft-spoken, perceptive, and unshakably kind; the type who disarms nobles by listening instead of arguing

- Background:

Seraphina was the “human warmth” inside a house built on strategy—she taught her children that power without restraint becomes cruelty.

Her illness began as quiet exhaustion and recurring fever, then progressed into relentless weakness, pain, and wasting—healing magic could soothe symptoms but never stop the growth consuming her.

In that era it was not understood; some healers suspected a lingering curse, others called it a “body-bloom” sickness. Decades later, it would be recognized for what it truly was: cancer—an enemy without a name in their time.

Her death left two scars: Gilbert’s drive to become strong enough to protect what matters, and Emilia’s loneliness masked behind composure.
