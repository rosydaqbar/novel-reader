Age: Early 30s

Appearance: Tall, sandy-blond hair tied neatly behind his head, gray-blue eyes, and a composed face that rarely gives much away; wears polished armor marked with House Halberg’s crest, a silver hawk over twin swords, and carries himself with the upright discipline of a man raised to command

Personality: Disciplined, cautious, and formal; values order, competence, and restraint over pride or theatrics; observant enough to notice what others hide, but careful about what he says aloud; beneath his steady command presence, he carries an old regret that makes him more sympathetic than he first appears

Background: Lucen was born into House Halberg, a military noble family tied to regional security and frontier command. He grew up under strict expectations, trained from an early age to lead soldiers, manage crisis, and represent his house without weakness. That upbringing made him dependable in the field, but it also taught him to place duty above personal desire. Years before the story, he fell in love with a commoner and failed to protect that bond from his family’s pressure, which left him trapped in a politically acceptable life he never fully chose. By the time he enters the story, he has become the sort of commander nobles trust and soldiers follow: calm, capable, and burdened by choices he cannot undo.

Story Use: Lucen functions as the military authority who expands the scale of the story beyond Elrond’s home. He turns the damaged magic stone incident from a local problem into a regional threat, leads the eastern expedition, and helps frame the frontier as part of a larger political and defensive structure. On a character level, he works as a foil to Aldric. Where Aldric chose love and paid the price for it, Lucen chose duty and lost something he still carries. That contrast gives him more weight than a simple commander role and makes him useful both in action scenes and in quieter conversations about class, sacrifice, and the kind of life a man ends up building.
