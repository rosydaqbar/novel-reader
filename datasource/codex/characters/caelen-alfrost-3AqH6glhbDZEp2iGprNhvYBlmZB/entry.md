- Relation: Emilia & Gilbert’s father; current Head of the Alfrost family; Wilhelm’s elder brother

- Titles: Head of House Alfrost; Grand Strategist of the Crown’s Southern Command (court-appointed)

- Age: Early–mid 40s

- Appearance: Jet-black hair with silver at the temples; sharp dark eyes; tall, disciplined posture; always seen with signet ring and formal gloves

- Personality: Calm, calculating, quietly intimidating; speaks gently but never wastes words; protective through preparation, not affection

- Background:

House Alfrost is known as “nobles of strategy and leadership, deeply tied to the crown,” and Caelen embodies that reputation.

He molded Gilbert into the visible spear of the family (military prestige and stability) while keeping Emilia trained for court survival—observation, patience, and ruthless clarity beneath polite manners.

After his wife’s slow death, Caelen became obsessed with prevention: he funds Academy research, builds quiet healer networks, and treats “miracles” as liabilities—anything unexplained is either politics or a trap.
