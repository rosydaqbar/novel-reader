- Allegiance (hidden): Operative of KAER “The War-Sower” (one of Kaer’s underlings assigned to the Althania operation)

- Cover Identity: Court Notary / Contract Auditor attached to the Second Prince’s office

- Age: Early 30s

- Appearance: Neatly combed black hair, thin spectacles, calm tired eyes; always carries a leather document tube and a small wax-seal kit

- Personality: Mild, agreeable, unremarkable on purpose; speaks carefully, never raises his voice; becomes frighteningly decisive when corners appear

- Background: Jaren was born a capable minor noble—respected, well-connected, and already known as a rising strategist—until the court turned on his house to protect greater names, stripping their crest and rewriting the truth until even old allies treated him like a contagion; his mother then wasted away from an incurable sickness no healer could explain, and his sister vanished under “protective custody,” leaving him with nothing but sealed lies and a mansion full of echoes. With his pride broken and his future erased, he walked to the edge of ending his own life—until a man he had never met intervened, spoke to him like his suffering had value, and offered him one thing the world refused to give: a purpose that could never be taken away. From that night, Jaren stopped trying to regain his place in society—and started learning how to move society itself.
