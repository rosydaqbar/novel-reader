- Position: Household servant (assigned as a “quiet errand runner / stable-hand”); secretly an undercover warrior

- Appearance: Sun-browned skin, black hair kept short, average-looking by design; moves like he’s always balancing weight in his hands

- Personality: Friendly enough to be forgettable; speaks in simple words; becomes ice-cold when duty activates

- Background:

Rowan is a planted shield: a veteran warrior embedded in the mansion to respond before formal guards can even be summoned.

Officially he delivers parcels, handles stables, and escorts maids at night. Unofficially he tracks suspicious hires, tests door seals, and intercepts threats meant for Emilia and Gilbert.

Only the Head of House, Madam Odette, and a tiny inner circle know what he truly is; to everyone else, he’s just “a reliable servant.”

He was placed here because noble houses draw assassinations and attacks—and House Alfrost answers that reality by turning even servants into contingency.

- Notable Ability:

Shadow-Guard Spearwork — fights with a short spear/staff (easy to disguise as a tool), specializing in silent takedowns and corridor control.
