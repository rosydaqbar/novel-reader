- Family: House Edevane (high-rank noble line of Arbalest; historically tied to civil administration and Academy governance)

- Position: Head of the Royal Academy of Arbalest (Grand Master)

- Appearance: A middle-aged aristocrat with a pronounced potbelly and soft cheeks; neatly groomed mustache, heavy-lidded eyes, and immaculate tailoring; wears rich layered formal robes beneath a fitted coat, carrying a decorative cane as a mark of status rather than support

- Personality: Dignified, proud, intensely proper; speaks with slow certainty; values reputation, tradition, and orderly excellence above raw talent

- Background: A non-combatant noble who rose through policy and institutional control rather than battle; he treats the Academy as a pillar of Arbalest legitimacy and guards it with ceremony, rules, and influence instead of force.
