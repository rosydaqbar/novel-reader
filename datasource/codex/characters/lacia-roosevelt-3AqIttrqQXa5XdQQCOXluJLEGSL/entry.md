- Position: Royal Academy staff (Enrollment & Records Office); the staff member who processes and approves Elrond’s enrollment

- Appearance: Neatly kept light hair (often tied back for work), clear attentive eyes, always holding a clipboard or record ledger; calm professional posture that makes nervous applicants settle down

- Personality: Polite, efficient, and quietly sharp; speaks gently but notices everything; not cold—just precise

- Divine Blessing:

Divine Clairvoyance — an innate sight that reads through concealment:

- detects hidden mana quantity and flow

- perceives the true nature of a being (disguises, abnormal traits, “wrongness” in magic)

- catches intent-fractures (when someone’s words and inner motive don’t align)

- Background:

Lacia is a Roosevelt by blood, born from a concubine line, and the half-sister of Julian Roosevelt (their ages only one year apart); despite the complicated household status, Julian still openly treats her as his sister.

Rather than chasing noble prestige, she chose Academy service, where her Blessing is genuinely useful—screening applicants, identifying forged credentials, and quietly preventing dangerous individuals from entering under false identities.

She is one of the first adults to realize Elrond’s mana is abnormal even when he tries to be “ordinary,” and she helps him enroll without turning it into a spectacle.
