- Family: House Bramble-Field, merchant-noble line specializing in medicinal exports.

- Position: C-Class Student. Great Magician with Restoration/Alchemical affinity.

- Appearance: Honey-colored hair kept in a meticulous bun, warm amber eyes, and a vintage watering can etched with protective runes.

- Personality: Enthusiastic and charming. She coordinates the circle's social activities to ensure they appear harmless to the faculty.

- Botany Interest:

Studies rare curative herbs intended for the Ashen Pulse. She claims to be searching for a cure for the King's illness, a pursuit that resonates with Emilia's secret grief over her mother's death.

- Puppet Status:

Hazel is the Logistics Puppet. Her merchant-noble background allows her to move research materials in and out of the Academy without suspicion.

She facilitates the discreet transport of biological catalysts needed for Mirelle Veyra and the High Spirit Nana to maintain their presence within the Academy's wards.
