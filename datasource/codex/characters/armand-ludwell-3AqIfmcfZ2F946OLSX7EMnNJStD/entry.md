- Position: Head of House Ludwell (Alfrost branch family); Alfrost Court Liaison for “discreet agreements” and refuge-related contracts

- Appearance: Tall, refined build; neatly kept dark hair; sharp, tired eyes that look like they’ve read too many letters with bad endings; usually dressed in quiet, high-quality formalwear with minimal jewelry

- Personality: Measured, diplomatic, and unnervingly patient; proud but never loud; carries gratitude like a debt and responsibility like a collar

- Background:

Armand was a true noble before exile—trained in diplomacy and crisis negotiation—until Ludwell lost their homeland to a foreign conflict and arrived in Althania with nothing but surviving retainers and a name that still carried risk.

He negotiated Ludwell’s acceptance under House Alfrost through layered terms of service: loyalty, discretion, and taking on roles where the main house could not afford exposure.

He authorized harsh contingency plans to prove Ludwell’s worth, including the early “substitute protocol” around Cestina—then spent years trying to ensure his daughter was still treated as a person, not a tool, especially after Emilia rejected the arrangement.

In the present, Armand’s skill is “winning without drawing blood”: he turns threats into paperwork, enemies into obligations, and scandal into silence—quietly protecting Ludwell’s place inside Alfrost while fearing the day the past comes to collect.
