- Status: Commoner

- Position: Royal Academy S-Class student (Elrond’s classmate) — Magic Genius

- Appearance: Short ash-brown hair, quiet gray eyes, slim build; plain uniform worn neatly with no noble accessories

- Personality: Calm, observant, and detached; rarely reacts to provocation and doesn’t care about social games

- Background:

Discovered young and recruited under the patronage of a member of Arbalest’s Magic Council, Rowan was invited into formal study before most nobles even touch real grimoires.

By age 12, he had learned and performed basic, advanced, and elite grimoires with frightening consistency; by 15, he is already studying forbidden magic—less out of rebellion, more out of pure capability and curiosity.

He keeps his head down, not because he’s timid, but because other people’s pride is simply inefficient.
