Higher-dimensional entities who oversee reincarnation and the balance between worlds.

- Purpose: To guide souls into new lives when their previous ones have reached an endpoint of destiny or consequence.

- Reincarnation Process:

When a person is resurrected by the Divine Beings, their memories of their previous life are completely erased. However, they receive something known as a Divine Blessing — a gift reflecting the essence of their past life's talent or virtue.

These Blessings often manifest as innate abilities, instincts, or affinities, though the recipients rarely understand their origin. They only retain a vague “feeling” of familiarity toward their skill or strength.

- Elrond/Saitou’s Exception:

Among the countless reincarnated souls, Elrond von Astrea is a unique anomaly. Due to his photographic memory, his mind resisted the erasure process, allowing him to retain every memory from his past life as Saitou.

This unprecedented event deeply unsettled many Divine Beings, as such memory retention was believed impossible. Most viewed it as a breach of natural law—an imbalance to the reincarnation cycle.

However, the Divine Being who personally resurrected Saitou opposed their judgment, declaring:

“You are the only hope.”

The meaning of “hope” remains a mystery, known only to that Divine Being. Whether it refers to Elrond’s role in the new world or a greater purpose tied to both realms is unknown.
