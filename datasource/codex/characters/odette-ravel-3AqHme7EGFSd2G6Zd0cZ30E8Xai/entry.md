- Position: Head Maid of House Alfrost (staff commander; household security coordinator)

- Appearance: Silver hair in a severe knot; posture like a blade in its sheath; always carries a ring of keys and a rosary-like chain of ward-charms

- Personality: Unshakable, formal, terrifyingly observant; praises rarely, punishes quietly; treats the mansion like a battlefield map

- Background:

Odette served in the royal district before being “requested” by House Alfrost—officially for excellence, unofficially because Alfrost households attract attention and threats.

She created the “Grey Apron Doctrine”: every maid learns escape routes, knife retention, and emergency signals; every corridor has a silent code; every tray, curtain, and candle has a purpose.

She personally trained Eira, and she keeps Brina close because “a girl survives longer when she sleeps.”

In House Alfrost—nobles of strategy and leadership tied closely to the crown—Odette is the invisible logistics that keeps the family alive. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

- Notable Ability:

Ward-Thread Seals — installs compact barrier seals into embroidery patterns (curtains, cuffs, rugs) to alert intrusion without obvious magic.
