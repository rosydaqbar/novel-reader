- Family: Ashfeldt (vassal house under Alfrost)

- Appearance: Long red hair, crimson eyes, bright and expressive

- Personality: Cheerful, lively, emotional balance in her group

- Background :

Lisoletelle was assigned early as a “companion” in name but a guardian in truth—Ashfeldt’s duty is protection, and she was raised beside Emilia to learn her habits, moods, and vulnerabilities before court life could turn trust into weakness.

Even as the four girls (Emilia, Cestina, Claire, and Lisoletelle) became genuinely close friends, Lisoletelle never forgets her job: she watches hands, distances, exits, and tone shifts like instinct.

Emilia never treats her like an assigned shield or a servant; she treats her like a friend, and that single difference turns Lisoletelle’s loyalty personal—she protects Emilia not because she must, but because Emilia is the first noble who never looked down on the people expected to bleed for her.
