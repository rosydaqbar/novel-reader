- Title: Demon Lord (one of the ruling demon sovereigns; distinct from the True Demon Lord of the past)

- Domain: Bene — a demon-state in the northern demon lands, among the closest to the buffer desert bordering elf and human territories

- Position: Ruling sovereign of Bene; wielder of a demon greatsword-class armament

- Appearance:

Red, half-curly hair worn slightly long; dark eyes with yellow pupils (the most obvious demon tell); otherwise human-like features.

Dresses in noble-style demon court attire—high-collared coats, layered fabrics, and metal accents suited for both court and battle.

- Personality:

Calm and composed, rarely raising his voice.

Lacks judgment in critical moments—he can be clever and informed, yet still choose the wrong path because he underestimates consequences or misreads “human-type” motives.

Not impulsive in emotion, but reckless in decision-making.

- Background:

After the True Demon Lord’s defeat, the demon lands reorganized into state-like territories with multiple ruling Demon Lords—powerful sovereigns who govern regions and maintain demon authority without claiming the True Demon Lord’s throne.

Azazeal rules Bene, a frontier-facing demon-state whose proximity to the buffer desert makes it a frequent pressure point in demon–elf–human tension.

- Weapon:

Greatsword — a heavy demon-forged blade suited to overwhelming strikes and wide-area dark magic release.

- Magic / Feats:

Dark Magic Manipulation:

- Forms massive constructs (giant hands, spikes, lances, grasping restraints) from condensed dark magic.

- Contact effect: his dark magic “seduces” living mana—pulling at it, draining it, destabilizing control, and making victims feel a dangerous, addictive weakness.

- Battlefield control: spikes and hands can shape terrain, pin targets, and break formations.

Arcane Versatility:

- Despite specializing in dark magic, he has unusually broad magic aptitude and can use multiple schools competently when needed.

- Combat Style:

Heavy pressure frontliner who turns the field into a trap—greatsword threat up close, dark constructs to punish escape, and mana-seduction to exhaust opponents over time.
