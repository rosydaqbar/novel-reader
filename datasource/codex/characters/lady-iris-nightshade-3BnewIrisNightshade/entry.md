- Family: House Nightshade, Highland border-march nobility.

- Position: S-Class Student. Specializes in Alchemical Toxin Magic.

- Appearance: Pale, aristocratic features with dark-violet hair tied in a sharp, high ponytail. Her eyes are the color of bruised plums.

- Personality: Analytical and clinical. She speaks with a monotone cadence that makes her observations feel like medical verdicts.

- Botany Interest:

Studies Mana-Reactive Neurotoxins found in predatory flora. She lures Emilia by offering to study how plant-based stabilizers can prevent mana fatigue, knowing Emilia's history with the condition.

- Puppet Status:

Iris is the Primary Vessel in the circle. Because she is in the S-Class, she has the closest access to Emilia during lectures and training.

Veyra uses Iris's high mana capacity to act as a relay for long-distance control.
