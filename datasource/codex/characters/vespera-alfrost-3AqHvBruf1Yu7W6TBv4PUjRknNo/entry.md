- Position: House Alfrost Magic Teacher (private tutor for Emilia & Gilbert; occasional Royal Academy guest instructor)

- Relation: Caelen’s cousin (daughter of Caelen’s paternal aunt — Caelen father’s sister); true Alfrost bloodline

- Age: Late 40s

- Appearance: Long dark hair usually tied with a silver clasp; pale skin; cool violet-gray eyes; elegant scholar’s look hiding a soldier’s balance

- Personality: Precise, stern, and relentlessly patient; praises rarely but always notices effort; treats “talent” as meaningless without control

- Background:

As an Alfrost, Vespera embodies the house reputation: strategy-first nobles deeply tied to the crown, but she expresses it through spellcraft rather than politics. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

Trained under Royal Academy ward scholars and later worked alongside court-grade protection systems—the same world where the Queen is Patron of the Royal Academy and Keeper of the Royal Wards. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

After repeated noble incidents (assassination attempts, infiltration, “accidents” staged as misfortune), House Alfrost formalized a doctrine: the heirs must be educated as targets, not children. Vespera was recalled from the Academy circuit to personally shape Emilia and Gilbert’s magical fundamentals—especially shielding, detection, and anti-curse discipline.

She is one of the few adults who can speak to Emilia without being “handled” by Emilia’s composure—because Vespera never argues emotions; she simply teaches survival.

- Teaching Style:

“Geometry before power.” She forces students to master sigils, timing, and mental stability first—then allows raw output.

Every lesson ends with a scenario drill: corridor ambush, poison mist, silenced casting, hostage warding.

- Notable Ability:

Rimebound Lattice — a cold-based ward-grid technique that can:

(1) map invisible movement through micro-frost reaction,

(2) anchor barrier layers without a loud mana signature,

(3) “lock” a room into controlled zones for defense or capture.
