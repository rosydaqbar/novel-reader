- Status: Commoner (slum-born)

- Position: Royal Academy S-Class student — Swordsman Genius

- Appearance: Black hair kept rough, sharp amber eyes, lean muscular frame; hands callused and scarred; uniform worn like it’s temporary

- Personality: Challenger type—direct, fearless, and stubborn; respects skill immediately and ignores status completely

- Background:

Bram grew up in the slums and was taken in by an ex-mercenary who lived by the sword; instead of schooling, he was raised on sparring, street fights, and survival duels.

He’s a natural absorber—he copies rhythm, footwork, and intent after seeing it once, then refines it by fighting anyone willing to test him.

He has faced countless styles and stitched the experience into his own; in pure swordsmanship, Elrond is still not a match for Bram.
