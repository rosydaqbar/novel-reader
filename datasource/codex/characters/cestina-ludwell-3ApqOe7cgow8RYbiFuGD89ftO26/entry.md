- Position: Emilia’s aide (but regarded as a friend)

- Appearance: Long silky blonde hair, purple eyes, graceful elegance

- Personality: Calm, reasonable, soft-spoken

- Background :

Cestina comes from House Ludwell—a displaced noble line that lost its footing in an overseas conflict, crossed into Althania, and was taken in as an Alfrost branch family under several terms of service.

She and Emilia became best friends since they were five, but Cestina’s upbringing carried a hidden purpose: her beauty and presence could mislead a crowd, and at formal events she was trained to mimic Emilia’s etiquette, handwriting, routines, and even speech cadence, so she could draw eyes away when danger moved.

Rumors in ballrooms sometimes mistook her as “the Alfrost daughter,” and the household quietly exploited that confusion—until Emilia discovered the truth at seven and refused it. She refused it for one simple reason: Cestina was never a tool to her—not a body double, not a guardian, not a shield—just her best friend, and Emilia would rather risk herself than let Cestina be used as a substitute.

Emilia then pushed herself into public appearances so substitution became impossible without scandal; after that, Cestina’s role was rewritten from “double” into “guardian,” keeping her close for protection without forcing her to be a sacrificial stand-in.

Later, her composure and quiet strength captured Gilbert’s heart, and she became his fiancée—not as a fragile noble beauty, but as someone who endured the stage meant to break her and still chose loyalty without bitterness.
