A subterranean fortress buried on the southern rim of the **Northern Wasteland** within a **"dead-mana" zone** that interferes with scrying. Its surface is disguised as a collapsed frontier fort. Internally, it features **looping geometry** to disorient intruders and houses the **Anchor Chamber**, a sealed hall containing a monolith that opens a portal to a **Pocket Dimension** for meetings of the **Seven Chaos Bringers**.
