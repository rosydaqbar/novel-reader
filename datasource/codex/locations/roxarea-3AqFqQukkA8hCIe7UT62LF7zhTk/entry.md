**The Crossroads Country** A small "crossroads" nation positioned at the northern buffer zone, ruled by the immortal Elder Elf **Serelis Thalanor**. It is a major trade nexus known for its fertile valleys and **irrigated orchards** of pomegranate-like fruit used for high-value liquor.
