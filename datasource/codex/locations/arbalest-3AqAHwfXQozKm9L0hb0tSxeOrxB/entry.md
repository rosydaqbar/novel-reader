The massive capital of the Althania Kingdom is surrounded by **towering white walls** and numerous watchtowers bristling with pennants. The city is organized into distinct wards, featuring pale stone roadways that are cleaner and smoother than those found on the frontier. The main entrance is the **Arbalest Main Gate**, where all travelers and carriages must pass over a **carved magic detection circle** that pulses with light to screen for dark magic, curses, or unknown energies
