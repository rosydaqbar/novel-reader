A notorious and unpredictable forest filled with **towering trees and tangled roots**. It is a dangerous habitat where monsters are often pushed out of the deep woods and into conflict with humans. Within these woods lies the **Secret River Clearing**, where a flat boulder overhangs a fast-moving river used by Elrond for chantless practice.
