A prestigious complex set behind tall white gates in the central ward. Key facilities include:
- **Main Building and Lobby:** A wide semi-indoor hall with high ceilings and stone pillars used for registration and orientation.
- **S-Class Homeroom:** A semi-circular classroom with tiered rows of seats rising toward the back.
- **Magic Training Area/Field:** A massive open space at least three times the size of a soccer field, equipped with **magic crystals** to maintain defensive barriers during spell practice.
- **Physical Training Area:** A wide stretch of hardened earth divided into stone-marked rings for sparring and drills.
- **Private Training Room:** A restricted facility containing weapon racks, practice dummies, and targets used for disciple-level training.
- **S-Class Dormitory:** A lavish residence that resembles a palace, featuring a lobby with a **large chandelier and polished granite floors**. Elrond’s suite includes a wide bed, a private bathroom with a large bathtub, and a balcony
