The **Royal Academy dormitory** stands near the main entrance gates, appearing more like a **stately noble residence** than a typical school building. Its orderly grounds feature a **small park with a fountain** and benches shaded by trees where students gather to talk or practice light spells. The interior begins with a bright entrance hall anchored by a **central reception desk** where staff monitor student ledgers. While standard student housing is located on the upper floors, the building is physically split; the **general dormitory sections** occupy one side, while the elite quarters are hidden away on the lowest level.
