1. Arbalest — The royal family, known for their magical bloodline.

2. Alfrost — Nobles of strategy and leadership, deeply tied to the crown.

3. Roosevelt — Scholars and swordsmen, masters of both magic and blade.



Other families:

* Ludwell: An Alfrost branch house known for court diplomacy and discreet influence, often used to secure alliances without open conflict.

* Ashfeldt: An Alfrost vassal house that serves as a loyal security-and-escort line, providing trained blades to protect Alfrost interests.

* Garfield: A renowned enchanter-blacksmith family famous for crafting magic-imbued weapons sought by nobles and the military (though their family is not noble).

* Sieghart: A prestigious noble house that backs Althania’s military through officers, training, and martial tradition.

* Silveria: A merchant-noble house within Althania that wields influence through procurement and trade networks, majorly controlling the flow of grain, iron, medicine, and contracts that keep nobles, the military, and the Academy running.

These three founding families stand as steadfast allies. Their unity and mutual respect form the foundation of Althania’s peace and prosperity.

However, although these families are the founding pillars of the nation, it doesn’t mean they hold the strongest power.

In the world of Althania, influence often shifts between noble factions, the military, and the mage academies — creating a delicate balance of authority maintained through trust, history, and shared ideals between the three houses.
