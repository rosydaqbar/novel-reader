- Beyond normal absorption, there are two special exceptions noted:

- 1) Magicians Who Work Directly With Raw Mana

- Some magicians can gather raw mana in the air outside their body and turn it directly into a spell before it settles inside them.

- Requirements:

- Very high focus, because they must sense and control mana in the air, not inside themselves.

- Fine control, since the more raw mana they handle at once, the easier it is for their control to slip.

- Risks:

- If control slips, the spell falls apart, because the raw mana is still chaotic.

- 2) Highlanders

- The Highlander tribe has an abnormal trait in their bodies.

- This trait allows them to:

- Manipulate raw mana more directly.

- Wrap raw mana around their muscles and bones to:

- Harden their bodies.

- Boost strength and speed.

- Change raw mana’s shape as a weapon.
