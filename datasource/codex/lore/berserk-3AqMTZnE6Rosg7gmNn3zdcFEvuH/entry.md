- Origin

- Berserk is a state that arises when a Highlander forces too much raw mana into their body at once.

- Effect

- When this happens:

- They start to lose conscious control.

- They attack on instinct.

- The state continues until the excess raw mana runs out, at which point their movements return to normal.

- Risk to Normal Humans

- If a normal person tried the same thing:

- Their body would probably tear itself apart before they ever reached that state.

- Highlanders survive because:

- They grow up training in thin air and harsh cold.

- Their natural strength can rival a human using triple Strength Enhancement, even without magic.

- That combination of natural strength and environment allows their bodies to endure being filled with so much raw mana.
