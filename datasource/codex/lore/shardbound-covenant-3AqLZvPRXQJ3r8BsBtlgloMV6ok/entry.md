Core Goal:

- To resurrect and claim the power of the True Demon Lord of the past by assembling the scattered “puzzle” of its essence, then use that power to dominate the world.

Origin Myth (The Hero’s Testimony):

- The True Demon Lord was defeated, but not fully killed; the final clash shattered its core power into countless fragments hidden across the world.

- The victory demanded the Hero’s life — the Hero died sealing the Demon Lord’s remaining will, leaving the party’s testimony as the only warning: if the fragments are gathered, the Demon Lord’s power can be reborn in a new vessel.

True Nature:

- The Covenant is not a cult of ignorance — it is an engineered conspiracy.

- It operates as a network of “ordinary” people (nobles, scholars, priests, merchants, officers) who each think they serve a necessary cause: “order through fear,” “salvation through sacrifice,” or “a world without kings.”

Supreme Leader:

- “The First Witness” (unknown demon; true name Nox)

- A demon said to have survived since the Demon Lord era.

- Lives among humans under a flawless identity.

- Rarely appears directly; most orders arrive as sealed directives, spoken through proxies, or delivered via dream/curse-messages.

- Public belief: The Witness is a myth used to keep members obedient.

- Reality: The Witness is very real, and watching.

- The Supreme Leader often appears only in a spiritual form during meetings with underlings inside the Pocket Dimension, keeping a physical presence off the board entirely.

Highest Rank: The Seven Chaos Bringers

- The Covenant’s apex council is called the Seven Chaos Bringers, led by the First Witness.

- During meetings:

- All seven wear masks and use ritual names only.

- They never “introduce” themselves or speak personal details.

- Location: Pocket dimension created by Nox

- The Truth:

- They already know one another’s real identities — the anonymity is ceremonial, a reminder that they serve Chaos, not ego.

- The masks exist to maintain a shared lie that keeps lower ranks terrified and compliant.

Reputation / Proof of Power:

- In past decades, whenever a Chaos Bringer has revealed themself in a town, that town becomes ruins in a single night:

- either by direct massacre, unnatural catastrophe, or by the civil war they ignite in hours.

- These incidents are deliberately left as folklore: survivors become rumors; rumors become fear; fear becomes recruitment.

How the “Puzzle” Works (The Demon Lord’s Fragments):

- The Demon Lord’s power is scattered as “Shards” — not just objects, but concepts:

1) Relics (weapons, crowns, seals, bones)

2) Places (sealed ruins, forbidden altars, “dead zones” where mana behaves incorrectly)

3) Bloodlines (marks that awaken under specific conditions)

4) Names (true words recorded only in broken scripture)

- Each Shard is a “key-piece.”

- When enough key-pieces are gathered, the Covenant can attempt:

- “The Reassembly Rite” — forcing the Demon Lord’s scattered will to condense into a living vessel.

- The Witness’s endgame:

- Either become the vessel… or crown a controllable puppet vessel.

Organizational Structure Chaos bringer underlings (below the Seven):

- Curators:

- Fragment hunters, ruin delvers, black-market buyers of forbidden items.

- Choir (Ritualists):

- Specialists who can stabilize Shards and prevent them from devouring the carrier.

- Ash Agents:

- Infiltration and provocation: sabotage, false-flag attacks, rumor warfare.

- Tithe Hands:

- Logistics: money laundering, procurement, shipping, bribery.

- Candles (lowest rank):

- Disposable followers who don’t know the real goal; they’re fed a “holy mission” narrative.

Symbol / Calling Card:

- A broken circle enclosing seven notches (for the Chaos Bringers), with a vertical slash through the center (the “un-killed” Demon Lord).

- Often hidden as an embroidery stitch, wax-seal defect, or coin scratch — designed to look accidental.
