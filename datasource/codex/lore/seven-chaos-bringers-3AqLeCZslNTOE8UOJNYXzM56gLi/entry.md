Primary Base Name:

- The Threshold Vault (also called the Wasteland Vault)

Location:

- Buried on the southern rim of the Northern Wasteland barrier zone, inside a “dead-mana” region where long-range detection and stable ward-scanning behave unreliably.

Type:

- Subterranean fortress-ruin built into the bones of an abandoned border-fort; expanded downward into sealed chambers and labyrinth corridors.

Reason this location is used:

- The wasteland already produces disappearances, monster storms, and mana anomalies—any incident can be dismissed as “wasteland loss.”

- The dead-mana environment interferes with scrying, tracking spells, and many palace-grade ward sweeps.

- It is close enough to Althania to influence events, but far enough that nobles rarely investigate without royal mandate.

Surface Disguise:

- A collapsed frontier fort half-swallowed by ash-snow and rubble, with warning markers left intentionally to discourage salvage teams.

Core Functions:

- Fragment holding vaults (sealed storage for Shards and shard-adjacent relics)

- Staging areas (quarters for select underlings, supplies, false identities, and “clean” travel sets)

- Containment chambers (for cursed objects, unstable carriers, and ritual failures)

- Conclave infrastructure (maps, cipher archives, and planning chambers built for silent coordination)

Access Protocol:

- Sevenfold Gate: the true entrance responds only to a specific “notch-seal” pattern.

- Each Chaos Bringer carries a corresponding gate component (mark, key, or seal phrase).

- The gate will not fully open to a lone user; it requires a minimum presence/sequence to prevent internal betrayal and to keep the Vault from becoming any single member’s private kingdom.

Internal Security:

- False corridors and looping stair geometry designed to break sense of direction.

- Anti-teleport lattice etched into load-bearing stone (to prevent direct intrusion).

- Silent alarm wards keyed to “outsider mana signatures.”

- Autonomous defenders (constructs, bound remnants, and other methods that do not require living guards).

Pocket Dimension Meeting Link:

- The Threshold Vault contains the “Anchor Chamber,” a sealed hall around a shard-reactive monolith.

- The Seven arrive physically to the Vault, then use the monolith to step into the Pocket Dimension meeting space.

- Nox may appear only as a spiritual projection within that space, leaving no physical trace in the real world.

- After meetings, the anchor is reset and the chamber is purged (wards, air, residue) to prevent tracking.

Logistics (how it stays supplied without exposing it):

- Supplies move through rotating caches along wasteland routes under the cover of “salvage caravans.”

- Only short segments of the chain know the full destination; most couriers believe they serve a frontier recovery program.

- The Covenant also maintains hidden bases in each major city across every country, compartmentalized safehouses used for recruitment, storage, medical/ritual support, and emergency extraction, all designed to look like ordinary residences or businesses.

Secondary Principle:

- The Threshold Vault is not meant to be “home.”

- It is a rare-use Conclave site: a place to store, seal, exchange, and vanish.

Optional: Personal Sanctums (each Chaos Bringer’s everyday “base”)

- KAER: a mobile war-room network (no fixed address; rotates safehouses tied to unrest zones)

- LYRIA: a high-society townhouse and private salon archive (information hub disguised as culture)

- BRONN: bonded warehouses and ledger vaults along trade corridors (logistics and smuggling control)

- VANN: a theatre-guild backspace and mask-workshop network (identity laundering and rumor shaping)

- SEREL: a “scholar residence” attached to diplomatic study work (cover for quiet rites and research)

- GHARUM: a hunting lodge and pack-den route system (movement through wilderness without records)

- MALACHAR: an inherited-looking manor with sealed sublevels (ritual depth hidden behind noble normalcy)
