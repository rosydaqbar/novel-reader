HOUSE SIEGHART AND BERSERK CONTROL

- Sieghart and Highlander Berserk

- The Sieghart household has spent generations training the Highlander berserk state.

- Their goal is to:

- Let Highlander-blooded warriors enter and leave berserk on purpose.

- Maintain control instead of being carried away by instinct.

- Because of this:

- Siegharts are considered dangerous even among Highlander-blooded warriors, since they can use berserk power without losing themselves to it.

- Sieghart Bloodlines as Proxies

- Sieghart bloodlines have already spread across Althania and Radras.

- They often act as proxies for the Highlander tribe.

- Whenever someone needs Highlander-type berserk power but cannot move a full Highlander clan, they:

- Turn to a Sieghart branch instead.
