A concealment spell that hides physical presence and makes the user blend into the environment, as if they were part of nature itself.Common Use: Assassins and bandits often use similar techniques to approach prey without being noticed.Limit: It hides physical presence, not mana signature. Any slight leak of mana can expose the user to someone with sharp senses, heightened perception, or sensory magic.Current Scene Function: Elrond uses it to follow Emilia into the academy woods while avoiding suspicion, but he remains cautious because using other spells could expose him.
