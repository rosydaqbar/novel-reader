Kaer's Objective:

- Turn Althania into a long-burning war vessel to harvest souls at scale and accelerate Demon Lord fragment reassembly.

Operational Layers:

Layer A — The Ink-Broker (the only recurring “face” near the Second Prince)

- Codename: “The Ink-Broker”

- Real name + lineage: Joren Caldra — House Caldra (obscure notary-bloodline)

- Function:

- sits near the Second Prince’s faction as a clerk/advisor/fixer

- engineers scandals, forges proof, redirects funds, arranges disappearances

- converts “political moves” into casualties and emergency powers

Layer B — Cells (compartmentalized tools)

- Ash Agents: riots, false-flag attacks, targeted killings staged as accidents

- Tithe Hands: laundering, procurement sabotage, bribery pipelines, transport permits

- Curators: auctions/ruins/black market fragment retrieval

- Choir-handlers: fragment stabilization and rite-prep hidden behind “healing” and “ward maintenance”

Kaer’s Strategy (Control the throne by breaking the country):

1) Split the royal factions permanently:

- Undermine the First Prince’s coalition (legitimacy, stability, loyal institutions).

- Inflate the Second Prince’s coalition (ambition, desperation, ruthlessness).

2) Convert politics into casualties:

- Trigger border incidents, riots, and “accidental” supply failures that force armed responses.

- Escalate into civil conflict where every victory creates more enemies than it removes.

3) Harvest souls under the cover of war:

- Mass death becomes “normal,” letting rituals and fragment extraction happen unnoticed.

Containment Logic (if any piece is exposed):

- A single layer takes the blame.

- Cells burn links, handlers disappear, paperwork contradicts itself.

- The story that survives is always smaller than the truth.
