- Safe / Internal Mana

- The mana most people use in spells is mana that their bodies have already made safe.

- A person can only hold a limited amount of this internal mana at once.

- This internal pool fills slowly, in two ways:

- By absorbing mana from nature over time.

- By taking in mana that someone else pushes into them.

- Raw Mana (Mana of Nature)

- Mana in the air and ground is called raw mana, the mana of nature itself.

- Raw mana is:

- Not settled into neat shapes.

- Still in a chaotic state.

- Because of that, it does not flow into a person’s body just because they wish for it.

- It must be:

- Absorbed naturally, at the body’s own pace, or

- Drawn in slowly and then converted into the safe internal mana pool.

- Elrond’s Exception

- Elrond’s body and mana control are trained far beyond a normal student’s.

- As a result:

- He can absorb much more raw mana at once.

- He can convert that raw mana into usable internal mana faster and in greater quantities than ordinary magicians.
