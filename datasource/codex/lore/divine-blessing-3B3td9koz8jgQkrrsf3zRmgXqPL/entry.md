Divine Blessing is a power category distinct from ordinary learned magic. In the current codex, blessings originate from the Divine Beings during reincarnation: when a soul is sent into a new life, its old memories are normally erased, but a Blessing remains as the distilled trace of that past life’s talent, virtue, instinct, or affinity. Most people do not understand the source of their blessing. They only feel that a certain ability is strangely natural to them, as if the body remembers something the mind does not. Because of that, a blessing behaves less like a spell someone studies and more like an inborn authority built into the person from birth. Some blessings sharpen perception, some alter the body, and some bend rules that normal magic usually has to obey. They can be passive, always active, or difficult to control, and in rare cases they interact directly with other blessings rather than following normal magical counterplay. The current material also shows that blessings are not limited to the natural reincarnation cycle. Artificial blessings can be branded into a person from outside, creating abnormal “gifted” individuals whose power is deliberate rather than fated. Politically, Divine Blessings matter because they distort the normal balance of strength. A single blessed person can change security, warfare, succession, and factional power. That is why figures like Darius actively gather blessed individuals, while others treat blessings with caution, secrecy, or reverence. Elrond remains the biggest anomaly so far: he possesses a Divine Blessing, but its nature is still unknown even to the Divine Beings, and his retained past-life memories make his case fundamentally abnormal.
